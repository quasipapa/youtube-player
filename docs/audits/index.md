# Audit records

This section contains dated investigations and verification evidence. An audit
record describes the trigger, scope, observations, decisions, mitigations and
remaining risk at the time of the review.

Audit records are historical evidence. They do not replace the current
requirements or operating procedures. Follow
[maintenance](../operations/maintenance.md) for the recurring dependency,
compatibility and security process.
